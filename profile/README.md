# FeDHLab – Federico II Digital Humanities Laboratory

**FeDHLab** is the Digital Humanities Laboratory of the **Department of Humanities, University of Naples Federico II**.

The Laboratory promotes and coordinates research at the intersection of the humanities, digital technologies, computational methods, cultural heritage and research infrastructures.

## Activities

FeDHLab supports and develops:

- digital editions and textual scholarship;
- computational and corpus-based approaches to humanities research;
- digitisation and digital cultural heritage;
- manuscript and book studies;
- digital archives, databases and knowledge graphs;
- IIIF-based infrastructures and digital collections;
- HTR/OCR and AI-assisted analysis of cultural heritage;
- research software and reusable tools;
- open and FAIR research data.

## GitHub at FeDHLab

This organization hosts software, tools, datasets, documentation and technical resources developed within or in collaboration with FeDHLab projects.

Repositories may contain:

- source code and research software;
- scripts, notebooks and computational workflows;
- datasets and metadata;
- TEI/XML, RDF and other structured data;
- ontologies and data models;
- IIIF manifests and related resources;
- technical documentation and training materials.

Large image collections and preservation masters are generally hosted on dedicated institutional infrastructures; GitHub repositories provide the related code, metadata, manifests and documentation.
